# BMV Finder User Guide

## Welcome to BMV Finder

BMV Finder is a comprehensive property investment platform that helps you find Below Market Value (BMV) properties, analyze market trends, and manage your property portfolio. Whether you're a first-time investor or an experienced property developer, BMV Finder provides the tools and insights you need to make informed investment decisions.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the BMV Finder website** at [https://bmvfinder.com](https://bmvfinder.com)
2. **Click "Sign Up"** in the top-right corner
3. **Enter your details**:
   - Email address
   - Strong password (minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
   - Full name
4. **Verify your email** by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. **Complete your profile** with additional information

### First Login

1. **Enter your credentials** on the login page
2. **Complete the onboarding tour** to familiarize yourself with the platform
3. **Set your investment preferences**:
   - Preferred property types
   - Budget range
   - Target areas
   - Investment goals

## Core Features

### 1. Property Search

#### Basic Search
1. **Enter a postcode** in the search bar
2. **Set search radius** (0.5 to 5 miles)
3. **Apply filters**:
   - Price range
   - Property type (house, flat, bungalow)
   - Number of bedrooms
   - Property features
4. **Click "Search"** to find properties

#### Advanced Search
1. **Click "Advanced Search"** for more options
2. **Set detailed criteria**:
   - EPC rating
   - Property age
   - Garden requirements
   - Parking needs
   - Transport links
3. **Save search criteria** for future use

#### Search Results
- **Property cards** show key information
- **BMV Score** indicates investment potential
- **Map view** shows property locations
- **List view** provides detailed information
- **Sort options** by price, BMV score, or date

### 2. BMV Analysis

#### Understanding BMV Scores
- **90-100**: Excellent investment opportunity
- **80-89**: Very good investment potential
- **70-79**: Good investment opportunity
- **60-69**: Moderate investment potential
- **Below 60**: Consider carefully

#### BMV Score Factors
- **Market comparison**: How the price compares to similar properties
- **Location analysis**: Area desirability and growth potential
- **Property condition**: Based on photos and descriptions
- **Market trends**: Recent price movements in the area
- **Investment metrics**: Rental yield and capital growth potential

#### Detailed Analysis
1. **Click on a property** to view details
2. **Review the analysis**:
   - Price comparison with similar properties
   - Market trends and forecasts
   - Investment calculations
   - Risk assessment
3. **View comparable properties** in the area
4. **Check market data** and historical prices

### 3. Portfolio Management

#### Adding Properties

3. **Enter property details**:

   - Purchase date

4. **Save the property** to your portfolio

#### Portfolio Overview

- **Performance over time** with charts
- **Individual property performance**
- **Market comparison** of your portfolio

#### Portfolio Analytics

- **Market analysis**:

- **Forecasting**:
  - Future value predictions

## Tips and Best Practices

### Property Search Tips
1. **Use specific postcodes** for accurate results
2. **Set realistic price ranges** to avoid missing opportunities
3. **Check multiple property types** to compare options
4. **Review recent sales** in the area for market context
5. **Save searches** for areas you're actively monitoring

### Investment Analysis
1. **Consider the BMV score** but don't rely on it alone
2. **Review market trends** before making decisions
3. **Check property history** and any issues
4. **Calculate total costs** including fees and renovations
5. **Assess rental potential** if considering buy-to-let

### Portfolio Management
1. **Regularly update** property values and information
2. **Monitor performance** against market benchmarks
3. **Diversify** across different areas and property types
4. **Review and rebalance** your portfolio periodically
5. **Keep detailed records** of all transactions

### Market Research
1. **Follow market trends** in your target areas
2. **Understand local factors** affecting property prices
3. **Monitor economic indicators** and government policies
4. **Network with local agents** and investors
5. **Stay informed** about planning and development changes

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: What is BMV Finder?**
A: BMV Finder is a property investment platform that helps you find Below Market Value properties, analyze market trends, and manage your property portfolio.

**Q: Is BMV Finder free to use?**
A: We offer a free tier with basic features. Premium plans provide advanced analytics, unlimited searches, and priority support.

**Q: How accurate are the BMV scores?**
A: BMV scores are based on comprehensive market analysis and are regularly updated. However, they should be used as a guide alongside your own research.

**Q: Can I use BMV Finder on my mobile device?**
A: Yes, we have mobile apps for iOS and Android, plus a responsive web interface.

### Property Search

**Q: How often is property data updated?**
A: Property data is updated daily from multiple sources including estate agents, property portals, and official records.

**Q: Can I search for properties outside the UK?**
A: Currently, BMV Finder focuses on UK properties. We're working on expanding to other markets.

**Q: How do I know if a property is still available?**
A: We show the last updated date for each property. For the most current availability, contact the estate agent directly.

### Portfolio Management

**Q: Can I track properties I don't own?**
A: Yes, you can add any property to your portfolio for tracking purposes, even if you don't own it.

**Q: How do I update property values?**
A: You can manually update values in your portfolio, or we can provide automated valuations based on market data.

**Q: Can I share my portfolio with others?**
A: Yes, you can share portfolio reports and analysis with family, advisors, or business partners.

### Technical Support

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: We support Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, use the latest version of your browser.

**Q: Can I use BMV Finder offline?**
A: The mobile app offers limited offline functionality for saved properties and portfolio data.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Use the "Report Bug" feature in the app, or contact support with details about the issue.
